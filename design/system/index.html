Language-aware typography

English headings keep the tight letter-spacing: -0.4px. Thai headings get 0 through :lang(th). Body line-height stays 1.65 (Thai needs ≥ 1.6 for tone marks and vowels). Fonts: Anuphan (headings) and IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Sans Thai (body), Latin and Thai subsets both loaded in src/main.tsx.
