ChampionWays ("we") lists competitions and puts students and other members in touch with mentors who help them prepare. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and your rights under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019).

## 2. What we collect

**When you sign up or sign in**
- Your email, display name, and password (stored in a form that can't be reversed)
- If you sign in with Google: the email, name, profile picture, and Google account ID that Google sends us
- Profile details you choose to add, such as occupation, organization, education level, and a short bio

**When you apply to be a mentor**
- Your full name, email, phone number, occupation, organization, experience, portfolio, and award evidence files, so our team can review your application
- The contact channels you choose to share (email, LINE ID, phone, Instagram, or another link; at least one) and your prices

**When you contact a mentor and leave a review**
- A record of which mentor you contacted, for which competition, when, and how far the process has gone
- Your star rating and comment

**When you submit a competition**
- The organizer's name and the contact person's name, email, and phone number

**Technical data**
- Cookies needed to keep you signed in (see section 7)
- Our hosting provider logs requests as usual, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the service secure

We don't use advertising trackers and we don't sell your data.

## 3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Create and run your account, let you sign in | Performance of our terms of service |
| Review mentor applications, show mentor profiles | Performance of contract |
| Show a mentor's contact channels to members who press Contact Mentor | The mentor's consent (they choose which channels to share) |
| Send email verification, ask mentors to confirm a consultation, send updates | Performance of contract |
| Require a verified email before contacts or reviews, and limit reviews, to stop fake accounts and fake reviews | Our and other users' legitimate interests |
| Show reviews and rank mentors (Rising Star) | Legitimate interests |
| Review competitions before listing them | Performance of contract |
| Comply with the law or lawful requests from authorities | Legal obligation |

## 4. Who can see your data

- **Anyone visiting the site** sees mentor profiles (first name and last initial, bio, what they help with, prices) and reviews (only the reviewer's first name)
- **A mentor's contact channels** are shown only to members with a verified email who press "Contact mentor" on that mentor
- **Mentors** see the first name of a member asking them to confirm a consultation
- **The ChampionWays team** sees mentor applications, competition submissions, and what's needed to run the service
- **Our service providers**, who process data on our behalf under contract only:
  - Vercel (hosting, file storage) · Neon (database) · Resend (email) · Google (sign in with Google)
- When you talk to a mentor outside the site (for example on LINE), that conversation is covered by that service's policy, not ours

## 6. How long we keep data

- **Account and profile:** until you ask us to delete your account
- **Mentor applications that were not approved:** 1 year
- **Mentor contact records and competition submissions:** 2 years
- **Copies of emails the system sent:** 90 days
- **Sign-in sessions:** expire after 30 days (or 8 hours if you don't choose "Remember me")
- **Email links:** single use, expire after 24 hours (email verification) or 14 days (consultation confirmation)

After that we delete the data or make it no longer identify you, unless the law requires us to keep it.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data and get a copy
- Correct it (you can edit your name, email, password, and profile yourself on the profile page)
- Have it deleted, or delete your account
- Object to or restrict its use
- Withdraw consent (for example, mentors can remove their contact channels at any time in the Mentor zone)
- Data portability
- Complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee

Send requests to `[privacy@…]` from the email on your account. We reply within 30 days.
